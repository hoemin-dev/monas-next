MONAS Pump, Progressive Cavity Pump Specialist

MONAS Pump is a manufacturer of Progressive Cavity Pumps and Dispensing Systems. Since 1998, we have specialized in progressive cavity pump technology and have supplied our products to customers in Korea and more than 20 countries worldwide.

We design and manufacture dedicated machining equipment for key pump components and continue to improve performance, reliability and maintenance convenience through continuous engineering development.
