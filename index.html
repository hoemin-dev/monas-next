The K-Series Progressive Cavity Pump

MONAS Pump is a manufacturer of Progressive Cavity Pumps and Dispensing Systems.

MONAS pumps are used across a wide range of industrial applications and are supplied to customers in Korea and more than 20 countries worldwide. With more than 25 years of experience, we continue to focus on reliable performance, ease of maintenance and long service life.
